Calculator

1. Select research

Choose Add research, pick Military III or Dragon Combat, and search by research name or stat. Add as many goals as you need, including goals from both trees.

Set Current level to your completed level, from 0–15, and Desired level to your target, from 1–15. Current level 5 and desired level 8 includes the costs of levels 6, 7, and 8. Remove a goal with its Remove button; its recorded progress stays saved.

2. Review requirements

Open Requirements beneath any selected research. Unmet prerequisites are included automatically. Enter a partial current level or check Achieved to record the required level. Uncheck to restore the level recorded before checking; if there is no previous checkbox action, unchecking sets the level to 0. You can always enter the exact current level directly.

Research progress is shared across all goals. A requirement needed by multiple goals is counted once, at the highest level needed. A completed rank proves a research has already been unlocked, so its earlier unlock prerequisites are not added again. If your selected research already has a current level above 0, its initial unlock requirements are satisfied.

When a prerequisite is also a selected goal, edit its desired level on that goal. The combined total uses whichever required level is higher. The requirements list shows that higher level so its checkbox matches the combined plan.

3. Check your Maester level

Enter your current Maester building level, from 1–40. Each research shows the building level for its desired rank. The summary shows the highest building level needed for unfinished research and prerequisites. Building upgrade costs are not included.

4. Enter boosts and compare costs

Open Cost adjustments · Edit boosts. Select Military 3 or Dragon Combat to enter separate boosts for each tree. Boosts apply only to research and requirements in that tree, even when your calculation includes both trees. Military 3 excludes Dragon Lore, Dragon Secrets, and Dragon Tomes. Enter your Research Resource Efficiency, Research Resource Reduction, and each resource's efficiency/reduction percentages. Open Material adjustments for research materials. Food, Wood, Stone, and Iron add Research Resource Efficiency to their specific efficiency, and Research Resource Reduction to their specific reduction. Total reduction is capped at 100%; materials use only their own boosts.

Reduced cost = original cost × (1 − reduction / 100) ÷ (1 + total efficiency / 100).

For example, a 20% reduction and 60% total efficiency change an original cost of 1,000 to 500. The totals include the selected research and any unmet requirements. Expand the per-research or combined breakdown to see where the costs come from. Research time is excluded.

5. Track missing resources and read stat gains

Under Original & reduced costs, open Your current resources and enter the whole amount you have of each resource or material. The Missing column subtracts these amounts once from the combined reduced total for all goals and prerequisites, showing zero when you have enough. Inventory is shared between both trees and does not change the research costs or Gains/Costs Reference. Update it manually after spending or collecting resources. Reset keeps your current resource amounts.

Use missing amounts as estimates: the game may calculate boosts with more precision than the three decimal places it displays. Differences can grow with larger costs. Keep a buffer and check the in-game cost.

Each selected row shows gains from its current level to its desired level. The combined stat gain list includes any extra levels required by the whole plan, counting each research level once. Open the Gains/Costs Reference guide below for the full level-by-level reference.

6. Save and transfer progress

Your goals, completed levels, requirement confirmations, Maester level, boosts, and current resource amounts save automatically in this browser. Reopening the same site in the same browser profile restores them. There are no accounts or automatic synchronization between devices.

Use Backup → Export progress to keep a copy or Import progress to restore it on another device. Import replaces your current profile. Clearing browser site data or using private browsing may remove progress. Older planner backups are supported.

Optimizer

1. Choose the tree, group, and goals

The Optimizer recommends research levels that fit your resources and give the best score for your selected goals. Select Military 3 or Dragon Combat, then General, Infantry, Cavalry, or Ranged. Troop groups include their applicable General bonuses. Dragon Combat also has Dragon Specific, with separate Dragon Defense and Dragon Attack vs. Dragon goals.

Check one or more goals and give each a different importance from 1–100. Selected goals cannot share a weight. New selections receive an unused weight automatically; duplicate entries show a message and prevent optimization until corrected. For the same compared gain, importance 2 contributes twice as much to the score as importance 1. A higher weight does not guarantee a larger displayed stat gain. Other branches can be included when they are required to unlock your selected research.

2. Choose how gains are compared

Plain gain multiplies actual added bonuses by their importance and adds them together. Percentage bonuses use the displayed bonus points: Defense +10% at importance 2 contributes 20 to the score. March Size uses added troop counts directly: +1,000 troops at importance 3 contributes 3,000.

March Size can dominate Plain gain when combined with percentage stats because their units differ. The optimizer may choose march research and show zero gain for the other selected stats. Use Remaining progress when you want those different units compared as relative progress.

Remaining progress divides each gain by that stat’s remaining research bonuses reachable at your Maester level, then applies importance. Those reference amounts exclude completed levels and ignore resource budgets. They are comparison totals, not gains you can afford or gains from the recommended plan. For example, +20% out of 200% remaining is 10% progress; +1,000 troops out of 10,000 remaining is also 10% progress before weights.

Neither mode guarantees a minimum gain for a selected stat or simulates combat damage. Applicable player, marcher, and SOP bonuses are added to the relevant troop stat.

3. Enter resources, boosts, and completed levels

Enter your available resources and materials, boosts, current research levels, and Maester level. These inputs sync both ways with Calculator. Military 3 and Dragon Combat keep separate boost profiles; resource inventory is shared. Boost decimals accept a dot or comma.

RSS kept (%) is set separately for each resource or material. It is the percentage you want to leave unspent: having 1,000 Food and keeping 10% leaves a budget of 900 Food. This setting does not change your saved inventory.

Current research levels are completed levels, not targets. Open Other research / prerequisite progress to record progress in other branches. The search respects research tiers, unmet prerequisites, every resource budget, and your Maester building level. Shared requirements are counted once. Building upgrade costs and research time are excluded.

4. Run the search and read the plan

Choose Optimize research. The search runs on your device; you can cancel it. Optimal for entered values means the best score was proved for your settings within numerical tolerances. Best found means the search stopped before proving optimality; a better plan may exist.

The stat cards show added bonuses from the recommended plan. Open How goals are compared for the score explanation and reference totals. Open Research Order for a valid prerequisite sequence, then Costs & stat gains beneath an item for its breakdown.

The cost table shows Spend, Left, and RSS kept. Left includes the amounts you kept unspent. Spend is rounded up and leftover amounts down for display. Equal-score plans may spend differently. Changing an input makes the previous recommendation out of date; run the search again.

5. Transfer the plan to Calculator

Use plan in Calculator opens a warning. Choose Cancel to keep your manual plan, or Replace Calculator plan to replace all manual research selections and desired levels with the recommendation. It does not spend resources or mark research completed.

Your input settings save automatically and are included in backups. Recommended plans are regenerated after reopening. Costs remain estimates because the game may use more boost precision internally than it displays. Keep a buffer and check in-game costs.

Gains/Costs Reference

1. Choose a tree and filter

Gains/Costs Reference is a lookup for research bonuses and original costs, independent of your Calculator or Optimizer plan. Select Military 3 or Dragon Combat. Each tree shows only its applicable resource and material columns.

Use Troop scope to show Infantry, Cavalry, Ranged, General, or All scopes. Troop filters also include General troop research and march sizes. Use Research or stat to search the level table by name or bonus.

2. Read the total costs

Total costs to maximum level adds original costs from level 0 to each included research’s maximum, currently 15. With a troop filter, it includes that troop’s research plus General research and march sizes; All scopes includes all troop types and General.

Minimum prerequisites from other branches are included once, at the required level. Open Additional minimum prerequisites included when shown to see those extra requirements. The troop filter affects these totals; the search field only filters the level table.

These totals do not subtract your saved progress, apply boosts, or use your available resources. Use Calculator for a personalized cost estimate.

3. Read individual research levels

The level table lists levels 1–15. Cumulative stats show the total bonus at that research level. Level gains show the additional bonus compared with the previous level. Resource and material costs are the original costs for that individual level, before boosts and without prerequisite costs.

Scroll the table horizontally to see all resource and material columns, especially on mobile. Choose Show more to display additional rows. Level 0 is omitted because it has no research cost or bonus.

    Replace the Calculator plan?

    This will clear your previous manual research selections and desired levels in Calculator and replace them with the current optimized plan.

    Your current research levels, Maester level, available resources, and boosts are already synced between both menus. They will be used for the new plan. RSS kept percentages are optimizer spending limits; they do not reduce your saved inventory.

    Reset calculator?

    This will remove all selected research and set every efficiency and reduction boost to zero, for both Military 3 and Dragon Combat, including Research Resource Efficiency, Research Resource Reduction, and material boosts.

    Your recorded research levels, Maester level, and current resource amounts will be kept.
